What has to be rebuilt

New customer accounts are passwordless and hosted by Shopify, so theme-based login and signup customizations don't carry over.

Custom signup fields
Moved to customer metafields, with existing values migrated.
Consent and terms checkboxes
Recreated with the same wording. Existing consent records are kept.
Tags set at signup
Segmentation and wholesale logic rebuilt with Shopify Flow or an app.
Loyalty, login and wishlist apps
Checked against the new account pages, then re-tested.
Anything that mentions passwords
Customers sign in with one-time codes, so emails, help pages and sign-in links get updated.
