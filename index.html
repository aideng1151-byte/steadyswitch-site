I move Shopify stores off legacy customer accounts.

Shopify deprecated legacy customer accounts in February 2026. It hasn't said when they'll be switched off, only that a date is coming. The new accounts are passwordless and hosted by Shopify, so anything your theme adds to the login and signup forms won't carry over and has to be rebuilt first.

Send me your store's URL and I'll send back a one-page report like the one on this page, showing what would need to move. I put it together from your public pages, so I don't need a login. It's free, and it's yours to keep whether or not you hire me.

What usually needs rebuilding

Custom signup fields move to customer metafields, and I migrate the values you've already collected. Consent and terms checkboxes get recreated with the same wording, and your existing consent records stay. Tags set at signup, which often drive segmentation or wholesale pricing, get rebuilt with Shopify Flow or an app. Loyalty, login and wishlist apps need checking against the new account pages. Customers will also sign in with a one-time code, so any email or help page that mentions a password needs updating.

How the work goes

If you hire me, I confirm the scope against your theme and apps using admin access you grant, and you get one agreed price before anything starts. I do the rebuild on a staging theme, away from your live store. When it's ready, I switch the new accounts on at a quiet time with a fallback plan in place, then re-test everything.

What it costs

A store with nothing custom on its login or signup pages usually costs $500 to $1,500. One to three custom fields or apps is $1,500 to $3,000. Four or more, or any wholesale logic, is $3,000 to $5,000. Agencies can subcontract migrations to me at a fixed price per store.
